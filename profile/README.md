We are the Decision and Bayesian Computation Lab

We study the algorithms and computations that evolution selected to perform biological decision-making, with an interdisciplinary approach mixing statistical physics, Bayesian machine learning, information theory and experimental biology.

🔭 Research axes

🐛 Neuro & Larvae

🧠 The Drosophila larva: decision-making and neuromodulation

🕸️ Mapping neural circuits: the connectome
📊 Large-scale behavioural assays

🥽 VR & AI for medicine

👶 Fetal MRI in virtual reality
✂️ Segmentation, annotation and generative models
🩺 Support for medical decisions

〰️ Clinical EEG & AI

🌊 Generative models of raw EEG (diffusion)
🏷️ Label-free representations of brain states
🏥 Clinical decoding with ICU partners: coma, sedation, consciousness

🧰 One toolbox across all three: Bayesian inference, statistical physics and machine learning.
